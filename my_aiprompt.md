## 💡 Prompting Best Practices Demonstrated

### ✅ DO's:

1. **Be Specific:** "FastAPI for backend" not just "backend"
2. **Share Errors:** Complete logs help AI diagnose faster
3. **Confirm Progress:** "Started working" before asking next steps
4. **State Purpose:** "Hackathon today" sets urgency context
5. **Request Formats:** "ZIP file", "setup instructions", "demo script"

### ❌ DON'Ts Avoided:

1. ❌ Vague requests: "Make banking system" → ✅ Used: Detailed images + specific tech
2. ❌ Hiding errors: Would delay solution → ✅ Used: Shared full error logs
3. ❌ Assuming AI knows context: → ✅ Used: "Hackathon", "don't frustrate"
4. ❌ Moving on without confirmation: → ✅ Used: "Started working" before next step
